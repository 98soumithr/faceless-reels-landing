Questions. Short answers

What do I get?

A finished vertical history reel: a hook-driven script, fact-checked and cited, expressive narration, real archival footage, a cinematic edit, plus a cover frame, caption and hashtags — ready to post at 1080×1920.

Why doesn't it look like typical AI video?

Real archival photos, cut-outs, spotlights, period frames and dated timelines — a treatment chosen for what each line means. Not stock clips stitched end to end under music.

How accurate is it, really?

Every name, date and number is checked against tiered historical sources before narration. Each reel comes with a source report, because one wrong fact in the comments is what kills a history channel.

What kind of content can I make?

History, specifically — US and world. Formats include weird-but-true single stories, myth-vs-fact, and state-by-state series. More niches come after history is rock-solid.

Which platforms is it built for?

TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Reels are 1080×1920 — download them and post whenever you like.
